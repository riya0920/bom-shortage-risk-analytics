Manufacturing supply chain · shortage risk

Can the factory build its 13-week plan?

A factory makes 4 products from purchased parts bought from suppliers. This project runs the bill of materials, supplier lead times and a Monte Carlo simulation to answer: how much of the plan can we actually build, which parts will stop the line, which suppliers can we trust, and who needs to act first.

2 · What is stopping it?

The parts that gate production, and when to order them

Ranked by how often each part was the one that stopped a product in the simulation. The order-by date uses the supplier's P95 lead time (a bad-case delivery), not the average. Ordering to the average means arriving late half the time. Day 0 is today.
